My Solea Story started back in May of 2017 when I purchased a practice that had an erbium laser. That laser served as a really nice coat rack for a couple of years until I worked up the courage to start learning how to use it. My practice vision is centered around a superior patient experience, and I hoped I would be able to offer anesthesia-free hard tissue procedures.
